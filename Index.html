National Teaching Councll - GTLE

“The National Teaching Council is mandated by Education Regulatory Bodies Act2020 (Act 1023), section 60(b) to conduct examination for the licensing of persons who successfully complete teacher education programmes. In order to be recognized as a teacher in Ghana, one must pass the Ghana Teacher Licensure Examination (GTLE).”.
